Section Four

Our read on pricing

When a price is too high, the pattern shows up quickly: inquiries dry up, few of them turn into showings, and the prospects who do tour raise the rent as an objection. This week we are seeing none of those.

  • Inquiries have held steady into the second week rather than fading after launch.
  • Showings are converting, at 39% of inquiries.
  • No prospect has raised the rent as a reason for stepping away.

Our recommendation is to hold at $2,950. There is no decision we are asking you to make this week.
